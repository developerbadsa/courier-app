01 System Overview

Shohnaat operates a high-frequency parcel distribution network requiring centralized operational coordination. This technical implementation unifies merchant order intake, warehouse sorting, mobile field dispatch, and automated financial accounting into a single robust platform.

02 Core Functional Modules

The platform is structured into six key architectural modules:

Customers & Merchants
  • Registration and profile management for clients and merchants
  • Dynamic shipping rate calculation by service type and destination zone
  • Payment methods, debt invoice statements, and automated settlement logs
Pickup & Shipment Management
  • Pickup request registration and automated rider dispatch
  • Warehouse inbound receiving, barcode sorting, and scale weight audits
  • Inter-branch transport manifests and automated daily delivery runsheets
  • Partial parcel delivery handling and reverse logistics (RTO) tracking
Delivery Representatives
  • Rider registration, route allocation, and mobile task queues
  • End-of-shift cash collection reconciliation and vault deposits
  • Representative commission logs and fuel advance management
Tracking & Notifications
  • Public web tracking interface with real-time milestone timestamps
  • Full audit log tracking every state change with operator IDs
  • Automated Meta Cloud WhatsApp and SMS status triggers
Merchant API & Webhooks
  • Direct integration with merchant Shopify and WooCommerce stores
  • REST API endpoints for automated bulk shipment creation
  • HMAC-signed webhook event triggers on delivery status updates
Financial & Accounting Engine
  • Cash on Delivery (COD) collection and rider liability tracking
  • Itemized merchant settlement statements and fee deduction ledgers
  • Double-entry ledger with profit & loss and balance sheet exports

04 Status Transition & Responsibility Chain

System state progression and assigned operational stakeholders at each milestone:

1. Order Placement Customer / Merchant
Shipment is created in the database and issued a unique tracking ID. Recipient receives an automated WhatsApp booking notification.
2. Pickup Assignment Dispatch Controller
The nearest pickup representative receives the task on their mobile PWA with address, parcel count, and time window.
3. Pickup Confirmation Field Representative
Representative scans the barcode label and confirms pickup. System transitions state to Picked Up.
4. Warehouse Inbound & Linehaul Hub Operations
Parcel is weighed on digital scales, sorted into regional linehaul manifests, and transferred to the destination depot.
5. Out for Delivery Delivery Representative
Delivery runsheet is generated. Recipient receives live tracking link with expected delivery window.
6. Delivery & COD Collection Delivery Representative
Parcel is handed to recipient. Cash on Delivery is recorded in rider wallet liability. Instant settlement statement issued.
Exception Handling: Failed delivery attempts, cancellations, and partial item deliveries are handled with reverse logistics manifests (RTO) returning items safely to merchant origin.
Operational Advantage: Real-time WhatsApp triggers reduce inbound support inquiries by up to 70%, while immutable audit logs ensure complete operational accountability.

05 Architectural & Security Principles

Enterprise design principles engineered for data integrity, financial security, and horizontal scalability:

01
Zero Cash Discrepancy
COD cash liability is attached to the representative upon delivery and reconciled against physical branch vault deposits.
02
Role-Based Access Control
Strict RBAC segregation ensures merchants, riders, customers, and admins only access authorized resources.
03
Mobile-First Field PWA
Representative interfaces are optimized for single-handed street operation with camera barcode scanning and offline caching.
04
Automated Daily Backups
PostgreSQL database snapshots run nightly with verified recovery scripts and point-in-time transaction logs.
05
Secure Payment Processing
Online transactions integrate with PayPal and Stripe SDKs. No credit card details are ever stored on platform servers.
06
Modular Extensibility
Built on a modular service architecture allowing seamless additions of AI route optimization and automated billing.

07 Phase 1 — Production MVP Deliverables

The core operational system scheduled for immediate sprint execution:

  • Secure authentication and dashboards for Admin, Merchant, and Field Representative
  • Customer and merchant profile registration with customized regional rate cards
  • Pickup request workflow: creation, approval, and geographical rider assignment
  • Automated unique tracking number generation and barcode metadata
  • Shipment state pipeline: Pending → Picked Up → In Transit → Out for Delivery → Delivered
  • Mobile PWA for representatives: task queues, recipient calling, and COD cash entry
  • Public customer tracking interface with milestone timeline history
  • Production deployment on VPS with automated nightly PostgreSQL backup scripts
Sprint Timeline: Phase 1 delivered in 7 to 10 days — a tested, production-ready system ready for live field operations.
